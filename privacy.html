These are personal websites run by me, Lukas Golino. No cookies, no accounts, no ads, and nothing is sold. Questions or requests: lukas.golino@hotmail.co.uk.

Visitor statistics

Every page loads two cookieless analytics services, Cloudflare Web Analytics and Umami Cloud. They record the page viewed, the referring page, browser, operating system, device type, screen size, language and country (derived from your IP address, which is not stored). Umami groups the pages of one visit with an anonymous identifier that changes every day, so visits can't be linked across days or to a person. I use this only to see which pages are read and how people move through the sites.

Hosting and delivery

The sites are hosted on GitHub Pages and served through Cloudflare. Like any web server, they process your IP address and request details to deliver pages and block abuse, under their own privacy policies (GitHub, Cloudflare).

lukasgolino.com only

  • Fonts come from Google Fonts, and the travel map loads libraries from cdnjs (Cloudflare), so your browser contacts those servers.
  • The contact form sends your name, email address and message to my inbox through Web3Forms. I use them only to reply, and keep them only as long as the conversation needs.

Legal basis and retention

Statistics and hosting rely on legitimate interest: running the sites, keeping them secure and understanding what is read. Contact messages are processed because you asked me to reply. Umami keeps statistics for six months; the other providers keep data as their policies state.

Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction or deletion of personal data about you, or object to its processing, by emailing me. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO).
